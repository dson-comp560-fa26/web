# Chalk talks

You must deliver at least one _chalk talk_ about your research at a lab meeting during your research project. A chalk talk is between 5 and 10 minutes long and uses no audiovisual content other than spoken word and handwritten whiteboard. Request a date on the Teams [chalk talk channel](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/channel/19%3A96677c0b079543aba91599f46a08469c%40thread.tacv2/chalk%20talks?groupId=cfb5d8f6-7b47-45af-ace3-e68372122ddd&tenantId=6232b055-76b9-4c13-9b88-b562ae7db6fb) -- usually a maximum two students per week.
