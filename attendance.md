# Attendance policy 

Attendance at the weekly lab meeting is required. Excused absences, when requested in advance, will be granted on a lenient basis. Unexcused absences or overall meeting attendance below 75% will likely result in a reduced grade. Students with a scheduling conflict for the weekly lab meeting should contact the instructor to make alternative arrangements.

The dates of lab meetings LM1 through LM12 are listed on the course schedule.
